Adding additional information

After adding a disc you may desire to add additional information to make subsequent search and retrieval easier.
  • 1. Select storage device
  • 2. Locate the disc
  • 3. Select the disc
  • 4. Select the Inspector panel
  • 5. Inspector Panel
1
Select storage device
To enter additional information, select the storage device from the side panel.
The contents of the device will be displayed.
2
Locate the disc
Since you generally add discs in incrementing values, click on the Slot column to sort the list by slot number.
If the most recent disc is not at the top, click the SSlot column again to sort in the opposite direction.

Select the disc you wish to add additional information.
3
Select the disc
Click and select the disc you wish to modify.
4
Select the Inspector panel
Open the Inspector panel
5
Inspector Panel
Change the indicated fields as you desire.
